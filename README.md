# US AI and Data Center Infrastructure — State of Play, November 2026

Research report with citations on the current state of AI and data center infrastructure in the United States (capacity, major builds, power and energy constraints, policy), and where AI and data-center investment and buildout could go if not in the US.

**Read the report:** [us-ai-data-center-infrastructure-2026.md](us-ai-data-center-infrastructure-2026.md)

## Headline findings

- US data center operating capacity is roughly 51–57 GW; Goldman Sachs projects ~64 GW by end-2026 and ~90 GW by end-2027, with power demand growing ~38% a year.
- The four largest hyperscalers are guiding to roughly 00–750B in combined 2026 capex, up ~77% from ~10B in 2025.
- The binding constraint is power, not chips: ~2,061 GW sits in interconnection queues with a ~5-year median wait; only ~28% of the ~1,066 GW developers requested is likely to ever be built.
- Announced capacity is not capacity: ~6 GW is actually running across the 18 largest US AI campuses, against ~30 GW announced.
- Outside the US, the Gulf states (Stargate UAE, HUMAIN, Qai) are the most credible alternative; Europe holds ~2 GW of AI compute (5% of the world total) versus 35 GW (78%) in the US.

Prepared November 5, 2026. All figures as reported by the cited sources; 27 sources listed in the report.
